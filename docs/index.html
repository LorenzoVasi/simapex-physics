The problem

Presets copy someone else's rig.

Every base, rim and pedal set behaves differently. A slider value that feels right on one rig is wrong on the next, and nobody can say why.

Hidden dynamics

Motor inertia, internal damping and friction change what reaches your hands. Most tuning ignores them.

Unknown scaling

The game's torque passes through gains, filters and delays before it reaches the motor. Without measuring them you are guessing.

Numbers without sources

"Real car feel" claims rarely say where their data comes from or how sure they are.

How it works

Probe. Measure. Identify. Calibrate.

A measurement workflow, not a preset generator.

01 · PROBE

Discover the rig

Read-only scan of HID devices and the simulator's shared-memory telemetry. Nothing is written.

02 · MEASURE

Run short tests

Small alternating torque steps, within your limits, while the wheel's angle is recorded at high rate.

03 · IDENTIFY

Fit the model

Least squares over Savitzky–Golay derivatives gives inertia, damping, friction, gain and delay, with confidence intervals.

04 · CALIBRATE

Match the real car

The real steering-torque chain is mapped onto your rim within safe limits, then checked with on-center indices.

The closed loop

No change counts until a new measurement proves it.

Measured data from your session meets reference data from the physics model and its sources. You apply the proposal yourself, because Fanatec and Thrustmaster expose no write API. Then a new session checks the effect.

The model

Your wheel base, as an equation.

A compact physical model whose parameters can be measured, not tuned by feel.

J·θ̈ + b·θ̇ + Tc·sgn(θ̇) = k·Tgame(t − τ)identified from P1 test data · tested on simulated data with known parameters
  • Jrotating inertia of motor and rim
  • bviscous damping
  • TcCoulomb friction
  • k, τgame-to-base gain and delay

Safety first

Your hardware, protected.

Direct-drive bases are strong. SimApex is built to stay well inside the lines.

  • User torque limit. Every test runs below a limit you set, enforced by a safety state machine with an immediate stop.
  • Backups before changes. Settings are saved before anything is touched, and can be restored.
  • Device protections stay on. No firmware changes and nothing invasive. Only supported interfaces are used.
  • Read-only by default. The current tools only observe: no force feedback is sent.
